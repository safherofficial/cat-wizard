How to play

Magic is
in the gesture.

Move through the hall, trace a glyph in one stroke, then release to cast. Time slows while you draw.

Movement
WASDMove the cat around the arena.
← ↑Arrow keys work too.
Cast a spell
HOLDHold the left mouse button and draw one continuous symbol.
RELEASELet go to cast the recognized spell.
